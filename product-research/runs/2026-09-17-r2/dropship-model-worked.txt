CASH AT RISK BEFORE THE FIRST SALE
  Inventory outlay                        $0.00
  This is the entire argument for the model, and it is a real one.
  What it buys in exchange: no control of lead time, no control of packaging,
  no unit to inspect before a customer sees it, and a refund that recovers
  nothing because the goods are in a customer's house and the supplier is
  8,000 miles away.

UNVERIFIED INPUTS declared in the spec:
  ALL SUPPLIER FIGURES ARE PLACEHOLDERS. Unit price, shipping, clearance fee and duty rate are not yet researched. The duty rate assumes the project's established 23.4% consumer-hardgood stack, which was derived for bulk import and may not be the rate applied to a single parcel. The $5.00 clearance fee is a guess at per-parcel brokerage and is the single most uncertain line.
