### What must be verified before spending

**Cast-iron citrus press @ $79 (achievable price)**
- Whether Section 232 steel duty at 50% applies to cast iron - this single question decides the product
- An 8-digit HTS classification from a licensed customs broker
- One factory quote at 500 units FOB, since $10.50 is an estimate from material mass
- A negotiated 3PL rate card, because published postage at 4 kg is the second largest cost line
- Whether a cold-traffic buyer pays $79 against an observed $72.19 incumbent
- Declared unverified: ex-factory $10.50; duty 23.4%; sea freight $0.80; postage rule; return rate; MOQ. Nothing has been quoted by anyone.

**Pet stroller @ $149**
- The packed carton dimensions - this product bills on volumetric weight, so carton size is the cost, and a supplier who packs it 20% smaller is materially cheaper forever
- Whether the wheeled frame or folding mechanism carries a live patent, which round one did not check for this item
- Actual return rate for wheeled pet gear, since 12% is assumed not measured
- HTS classification for a wheeled pet carrier
- Declared unverified: ex-factory $17.50; 0.080 CBM; packed dimensions; $170/CBM sea rate; duty 23.4%; a 12% return rate assumed from pet-gear size and fit complaints.

**Litter box furniture cabinet @ $149**
- The 18 kg billable-weight postage figure - it is extrapolated past the published points the rule was fitted to and is the single largest cost line
- HTS classification for wood or MDF pet furniture, which may not be the 23.4% consumer-goods stack
- One LCL quote, since 0.090 CBM at the top of the rate range moves this materially
- Damage-in-transit rate for flat-pack furniture, which drives both returns and resellability
- Declared unverified: ex-factory $19.00; 0.090 CBM; $170/CBM sea rate; duty 23.4% (furniture may classify differently); postage at 18 kg billable, extrapolated beyond the fitted range.
