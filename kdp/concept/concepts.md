# Concepts

Status: **NOT STARTED** — blocked on Phase 1.

Three concepts, each grounded in evidence from `kdp/research/market.md`, then one
recommendation. This file stays empty until that research exists, because a
concept invented without market evidence is just a guess with extra steps.

Direction agreed with the owner: the intersection of nature, botanical and
animal subjects with mandala and geometric pattern work, for adults, at medium
detail.

## Template for each concept

- **Working title** and subtitle
- **Promise** in one line: what the buyer gets
- **Buyer**: who specifically, and what they are choosing between
- **8 to 10 sample page subjects**
- **Differentiators**, each tied to a specific complaint quoted in the research
- **Why it might fail**

## Originality constraints

- Learn from the market in aggregate. Never reproduce a competitor's
  illustrations, cover layout, title, series branding, characters or
  distinctive page structure.
- No copyrighted or trademarked franchises, characters or brand marks.
- No misleading claims: no bestseller or award claims, no invented endorsements,
  no fabricated review quotes.
- Prompts name no living artist and no competing title.

**Gate 1: the owner approves one concept before any paid generation.**
