Maxwell Mnguni is a Vancouver-based model and athlete. He won the Red Bull Log Drivers competition in Victoria's Inner Harbour in 2026 — taking a field of 128 that included a Lumberjack World Championships competitor — and competes on the CANLOG circuit.

He works as a deckhand on tugboats out of the Lower Mainland, a job that shows in the build and in how he carries himself outdoors. Visible scarring across the chest and shoulder; tattoos on the neck and right arm.

Open to editorial, commercial, fitness and lifestyle work across Vancouver and beyond. Comfortable with physical direction, cold and wet conditions, water-based shoots and early calls. Booking is direct — no agency in between, no delay on a yes.
