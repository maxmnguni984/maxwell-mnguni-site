# Manual inputs

Amazon.com is blocked by this session's network proxy, so anything from Amazon arrives here by hand.

## Naming

`captures/<what>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.txt` or `.png`

The date is not decoration. A Best Sellers Rank without a date is meaningless, and a price without a date is close to it.

## What goes here

- Bestseller and new-release list pages
- Search result pages
- Book detail pages, including the Product details block
- Negative reviews, filtered to 1 to 3 stars, verbatim
- The two figures from the KDP royalty calculator

Paste raw. Do not tidy or summarise. I would rather extract from mess than lose a detail you judged unimportant.

See `../research/capture-sheet.md` for the exact URLs and the fill-in table.
