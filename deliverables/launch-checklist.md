# Launch readiness checklist

Updated 2026-09-17. Status of every item is real, not aspirational.

**The store is not launch-ready and will not be described as such.** Twenty-one
blockers are open. They are listed rather than summarised, because a checklist
that hides its failures is worse than none.

## Legend

- **DONE** — built and verified by a test that can fail.
- **BLOCKED** — cannot proceed without a named input from the owner.
- **NEEDS APPROVAL** — ready to do, waiting on a spend or publish decision.

## The twenty-one blockers, in the order they unlock things

1. Choose a product.
2. Supply a business entity name and registered address.
3. Supply a return address.
4. Supply a support email address.
5. Choose a brand name.
6. Clear that name against the trademark register.
7. Check domain availability.
8. Get a supplier unit price.
9. Get a supplier shipping cost to a US address.
10. Confirm DDP or DDU.
11. Confirm the HTS code and duty rate.
12. Confirm tracked transit time.
13. Confirm MOQ and variants.
14. Approve a sample order.
15. Inspect the sample.
16. Photograph the product.
17. Fill in real specifications and dimensions.
18. Approve a Shopify plan.
19. Connect a payment provider.
20. Place a test order in test mode.
21. Approve an advertising budget.

Items 8 through 12 are one supplier conversation. Items 2 through 7 are yours
alone. Item 1 gates everything.

## What I need from you, smallest first

**One decision:** which product. The recommendation is the car trunk organizer,
with the aluminium laptop stand as the alternative if you prefer its audience.

**Six facts:** business entity name, registered address, return address, support
email, brand name, country of registration.

**Three numbers, from one supplier page:** unit price, shipping to a US address,
and whether the quote includes duty. If those total under $13.65 for the trunk
organizer, the economics hold.

**Or one change:** run this where the network is not restricted to code
infrastructure, and the agents finish the supplier, delivery and demand work
themselves.
