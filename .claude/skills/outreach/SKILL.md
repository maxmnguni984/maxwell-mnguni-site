# Outreach

Booking direct means nobody else is filling the calendar. Outreach is the job
between jobs. The aim is a short list of people who plausibly book someone like
Maxwell, and a specific reason for each one.

## What actually differentiates Maxwell

Lead with this, not with "available for work":

- Red Bull Log Drivers champion, Victoria 2026, out of a field of 128 that
  included a Lumberjack World Championships competitor. This is a real,
  checkable result and it is the strongest line he has.
- Working tugboat deckhand out of the Lower Mainland. The build and the
  weathering are earned, not gym-made — that reads on camera and it is
  genuinely hard to cast for.
- Actually comfortable in cold and open water, in bad weather, at 5am. Most
  models are not, and outdoor brands know it.
- Books direct, so a yes comes back the same day with no agency in between.

He is 5'7". Do not pitch runway or high-fashion editorial that has a height
floor — it wastes his time and theirs. Aim at fitness, outdoor, workwear,
marine, lifestyle, commercial, and campaign work where presence beats stature.

## Who to target, roughly in order

1. **Outdoor and workwear brands with a BC presence** — the story fits without
   any stretching.
2. **Local commercial photographers and studios** who shoot lifestyle and
   fitness. They cast repeatedly, and one good relationship is worth more than
   fifty cold brand emails.
3. **Vancouver production companies and casting directors** for commercial and
   branded content. Vancouver shoots a lot.
4. **Fitness, supplement, and athletic apparel brands**, national or local.
5. **Marine, fishing, and industrial** companies needing authentic crew imagery
   — he is the real thing, which is unusual and worth saying.

## Research each target before writing

Never send the same email twice. For each one find: who actually handles
casting or brand marketing, what they shot most recently, and whether they use
real athletes or agency talent. If you cannot find a specific reason this
person would want this specific model, drop them from the list.

## Writing the pitch

Under 120 words. Structure:

1. One line naming something specific they made, and why Maxwell fits it.
2. Two lines on the differentiator above — result, job, conditions.
3. One line of logistics: Vancouver, direct booking, travels.
4. A link to https://maxwell-mnguni.vercel.app/ and the comp card.
5. One question that is easy to answer yes to.

No attachments on a cold email — link instead. No "I would love the
opportunity". No paragraph about passion. Subject line should be concrete:
the result, or the fit, not "Model available".

## Process

- Draft in Gmail; do not send anything without Maxwell reading it.
- Log every target with the date sent so nobody gets pitched twice.
- One follow-up after 8–10 days, two lines, no guilt, then stop.
- Track what gets replies and drop the categories that do not.

## Do not

Scrape or buy personal contact details. Mass-send an identical email. Claim
credits, clients, or representation he does not have. Misstate the specs — they
get checked on set and a wrong number ends the relationship.
