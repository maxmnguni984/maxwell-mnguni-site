# Booking enquiries

Maxwell books direct — no agency in between. The promise on the site is "no
delay on a yes", so speed of reply is the product. Aim to have a draft ready
the same day.

## Before drafting anything

`rates.md` in this directory holds the current rate card and the standard
terms. Read it first. If it still has `TODO` placeholders, ask Maxwell for the
numbers rather than inventing them — a wrong rate quoted in writing is hard to
walk back.

## Handling an enquiry

1. **Read the whole thread**, not just the latest message. Pull out: shoot
   date(s), location, usage (editorial / commercial / social / stock), how long
   the usage runs, territory, day rate offered if any, wardrobe, whether it is
   paid or TFP, and who the client actually is.
2. **Check the calendar** for the shoot dates and for travel time. Vancouver to
   Victoria is a ferry, not a drive — half a day each way. Flag anything that
   collides with a CANLOG event.
3. **Research the client** if the name is unfamiliar. A real brand, agency, or
   photographer has a footprint. No footprint plus an unusually high rate plus
   pressure to move fast is the shape of a scam — say so plainly rather than
   drafting a warm reply.
4. **Draft the reply as a Gmail draft.** Never send without Maxwell reading it.
5. **Log the lead** so the pipeline is not living in an inbox.

## Voice

Direct, warm, unfussy, no exclamation marks, no "I hope this finds you well".
Short paragraphs. Lead with availability, then the rate, then the practical
question that moves it forward. Confidence without hard-selling — the work and
the comp card do the selling.

Say yes to the shoot before negotiating the number. Attach or link the comp
card (`maxwell-mnguni-compcard.jpg`) when the client has not seen it.

## Always establish before confirming

- Dates and call time, in writing
- Rate, and whether it is a day rate or a half day
- Usage and term — "social only, 6 months" and "all media, perpetuity" are not
  the same job and must not be the same price
- Who pays for travel, and whether it is billed separately
- Deposit terms and the cancellation window
- Who is on set and where exactly it is

## Things to hold the line on

- Nudity or implied nudity: ask Maxwell first, every time. Never agree on his
  behalf.
- Cold water and open water work is fine and is a genuine differentiator, but
  safety cover on set is a condition, not a nicety.
- Buyouts get quoted separately from the day rate.
- Unpaid work only when Maxwell has said yes to that specific job.
